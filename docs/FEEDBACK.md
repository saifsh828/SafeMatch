## What We Heard (Themes)

1. **Explain journey before action.** Repeated requests asked for clearer onboarding, examples, and what happens after each step.
2. **Show disclosure before confirmation.** Users wanted summary of exactly what becomes public and what remains private.
3. **Make completion unmistakable.** Users asked for clearer success state, next action, transaction status, and error recovery.
4. **Protect small-screen usability.** Several users reported cramped controls or navigation on smallest phone displays.
5. **Keep selective disclosure.** Most positive feedback praised sharing only minimum required information.
