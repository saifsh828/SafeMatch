# SafeMatch Brand Brief

## Tagline

Prove what matters. Keep identity private.

## Key Messages

- Selective disclosure: apps receive the claim they need, not the identity data they do not.
- Verifiable trust: Midnight preprod confirms issuer trust, credential status, and replay protection on-chain.
- Human control: user reviews the exact public claim before wallet confirmation.

## X Profile Bio

Private age and identity proofs on Midnight. Share the claim an app needs—not your DOB, documents, or identity secrets.

## X Banner Concept

Dark Midnight field with one luminous shield split into two layers: a private credential layer hidden behind glass and a small public proof chip in front. Use mint for verified state, violet for zero-knowledge computation, and one concise line: “Proofs, not documents.”

## Level 6 Onboarding Message

1. Install and unlock 1AM, then select Midnight preprod.
2. Open https://safe-match-eosin.vercel.app/.
3. Connect wallet and confirm preprod network.
4. If credential status is missing, follow the synthetic issuer guide in `docs/USAGE.md`; never use real identity data.
5. Choose an age-range, verified-person, or combined claim.
6. Review the public/private summary, then confirm proof in 1AM.
7. Wait for the Midnight transaction ID.
8. Submit feedback at https://forms.gle/EN3ZuNWG33MNyqER9 with your public preprod wallet address. Never share seed phrase, private key, owner/provider secret, or identity documents.
